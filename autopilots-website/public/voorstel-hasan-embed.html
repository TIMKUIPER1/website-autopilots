Collaboration roadmap

Three phases, each with a clear purpose and trigger.

01

Phase 1: trust, support and handover

The first phase is about operational reliability. Existing clients need stable support, Leadfactor needs to be completed properly, and Autopilots needs confidence that technical delivery can be delegated.

  • Monthly support fee per active client starts at EUR 75.
  • From 10 active clients, the support fee increases to EUR 100 per active client.
  • Leadfactor support fee starts only after implementation and go-live.
  • Leadfactor completion fee: EUR 750 after successful delivery.
  • Implementation commission starts at 15% and scales up as client volume grows.
Starts now
02

Phase 2: technical growth and operational optimization

Once Autopilots reaches 20 active clients, the role expands from technical execution into technical strategy, systems thinking and operational automation.

  • Internal service fee starts at EUR 1,000 per month from 20 active clients.
  • For every 10 additional clients, the internal service fee increases by EUR 1,000 per month.
  • Focus areas: SOPs, reusable workflows, support automation, AI agents, quality control and technical roadmap.
From 20 clients

Commercial terms

The implementation commission scales with active client volume. This is separate from support and internal service fees.

  • From 20 active clients: 20% implementation commission.
  • From 50 active clients: 22.5% implementation commission.
  • From 75 active clients: 25% implementation commission.
  • Leadfactor completion: EUR 750 after successful delivery.
  • From 20 active clients: EUR 1,000 per month internal service fee.
  • After that: +EUR 1,000 per month for every 10 additional active clients.
  • Large new features, new integrations and rebuilds are scoped separately.

Calculation logic

Support fee = EUR 75 per active client up to 9 clients, and EUR 100 per active client from 10 active clients onward. The internal service fee starts at 20 active clients with EUR 1,000 per month and increases by EUR 1,000 for every 10 additional active clients.

The commission rate scales from 15% to 25% based on active client volume. This calculator shows monthly base compensation and commission percentage only. It excludes one-off project fees, large feature work, new integrations and custom rebuilds.

05
Scope definition

Clear scope protects quality, speed and the working relationship.

Included in support
  • Small fixes and small improvements.
  • Monitoring and technical checks.
  • Support for technical issues.
  • Keeping existing client environments stable.
  • Light adjustments to existing workflows.
Scoped separately
  • New features or major expansions.
  • New integrations.
  • Full rebuilds of client environments.
  • Urgent work outside agreed scope.
  • Product development beyond active client support.
06
AI-first operations

We want to automate our own operations while we automate client operations.

A key part of the long-term vision is building internal AI employees for Autopilots. One practical first step is an AI support agent that can participate in WhatsApp groups or support chats, recognize questions, retrieve context, structure tickets, flag priorities and prepare answers or tasks.

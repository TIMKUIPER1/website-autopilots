## Boundary

Every insert into `integration.health_events` now obtains a transaction-scoped
lock derived from its product connection. Scheduler and manual observations for
one connection therefore cannot interleave incident transitions. After the
lock, a new observation must have a strictly later timestamp than existing
evidence. An exact existing observation key bypasses the ordering check so the
established `ON CONFLICT` replay contract remains idempotent.

The guard has a fixed search path, is unavailable to browser roles and performs
no provider call. It does not delete or rewrite existing health, incident or
audit evidence.

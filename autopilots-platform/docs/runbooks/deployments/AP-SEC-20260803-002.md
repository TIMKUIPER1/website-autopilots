## Boundary

The migration enables RLS and revokes `public`, `anon` and `authenticated`
privileges on the fourteen empty legacy tables with no current runtime caller.
It adds no policy and changes no data. The active `sales-dashboard` service-role
mirrors—`audit_log`, `integration_health`, `invoices_sales` and `raw_imports`—
are explicitly outside this change.

## Rollback-only preflight

The exact migration body ran under the database owner in a transaction. The
probe asserted RLS on all fourteen tables and zero browser `SELECT` privileges,
then rolled back and returned contract
`autopilots.legacy-rls-rollback-probe.v1`. No residue or external write was
created.

## Boundary

`public.autopilots_brand_twin(profile_id, brand_slug)` replaces the managed
brand-detail fixture with a durable Supabase aggregate. The RPC requires an
active profile and exact legal-entity/brand membership. It reads registered
connectors, their latest governed health observation, active incidents and
measured/reconciled ledger entries. Browser roles cannot execute it.

Customers, goals, products, lifecycle definitions and external mappings remain
empty until separate authoritative projections exist. Revenue, allocated cost
and margin remain `null`; measured cost is also `null` when no qualifying
ledger entry exists. This prevents an empty ledger from being displayed as
verified zero cost.

AutoReviews may still add its bounded `aggregate_no_pii` snapshot at runtime,
but that adapter cannot replace the durable identity, scope, connector,
incident or finance fields in the twin.

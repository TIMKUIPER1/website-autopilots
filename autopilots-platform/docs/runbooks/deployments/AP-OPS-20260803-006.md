## Finding and correction

A rollback-only second-organization probe gave the existing owner one temporary
brand-scoped membership in a synthetic legal entity and created one synthetic
incident. The v1 portfolio incident function returned that incident because it
scoped by all current memberships for the profile, not by the legal entity in
the durable application session.

V2 requires `profile_id`, `legal_entity_id` and optional `brand_slug`. It checks
an active profile, current membership and legal entity, then applies the exact
legal-entity predicate to every brand and incident row. Runtime calls now pass
the organization from the resolved durable session. V1 server-role execution
is revoked.

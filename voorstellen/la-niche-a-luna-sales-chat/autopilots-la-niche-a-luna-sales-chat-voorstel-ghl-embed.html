FAQ

Important rules before the AI answers independently.

Can the AI give medical advice? +
No. The AI provides product information and buying guidance within agreed boundaries. Medical symptoms, uncertainty or risk situations are escalated to expert review or the customer is advised to contact a veterinarian.
Why is the expert intake important? +
Because website and product data alone do not contain enough context for sensitive product choices. La Niche à Luna’s expert nuance determines what the AI can say, cannot say and may only say under conditions.
Can the AI learn from new questions? +
Yes. New situations that the AI should not answer independently are escalated. The approved expert response can then be processed into the knowledge base so the AI becomes more complete over time.
Which language do we use? +
French is the primary language because the webshop and customer communication are focused on that market. English can be added as an extra language when commercially relevant.
